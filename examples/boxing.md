# Example: a boxing podcast edit

The brief needs a speaker, convincing boxing action and fast editorial graphics. Those jobs do not all need to be generated together.

## Split the work

| Layer | Plan | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Podcast speaker | Generate a focused speaking take | Exact words, face, natural delivery |
| Boxer action | Generate separate jab/cross/recovery shots | Visible contact, recoil and hands returning to guard |
| Camera punch-ins and blur | Author in the edit | Motion lands on intended words |
| Channel interface | Build separately in Remotion | Remains sharp above blurred footage |
| Animated words | Adapt a motion-library prompt to boxing copy | Finite readable movement, correct emphasis |
| Audio | Keep dialogue, licensed music and impacts separate | Punch sound lands on contact; speech stays clear |

An earlier action may contain motion but fail to show a meaningful punch. Diagnose the missing action. Ask for glove contact, bag recoil and guard recovery, then replace that shot while preserving the approved speaker.

For the sound comparison, show the same picture twice: dialogue only, then the complete mix. Do not change the image or talk over either pass. Use music and effects you are allowed to publish; this kit supplies no soundtrack.

Copyable request:

> Use my podcast reference's editorial rhythm for a boxing clip. Plan the speaking performance and action shots separately. Keep the interface, animated captions and camera effects editable in Remotion. Make the punch contact clear before adding impact sound. Show a short local motion test and the generation budget before paid calls.
