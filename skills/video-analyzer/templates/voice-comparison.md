Compare after matching loudness, at natural speed. If the user requests an ElevenLabs-only comparison, include only those candidates. Speaker-embedding similarity can help sort a larger set, but it does not prove the correct accent, emotion or a natural join. Do not invent a numerical pass threshold without calibration to the user's listening judgments.

For a voice replacement, separately inspect word timing and lip sync against the actual source speech. A voice-conversion model and a text-to-speech model are different tools; verify which one was used. Record every transformation and keep the original audio.
